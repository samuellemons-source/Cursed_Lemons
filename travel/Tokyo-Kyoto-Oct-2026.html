Walking radius by hotel

Things matching shared interests within roughly 5–20 minutes on foot (Hakone: walk / hotel shuttle / short cable-car hops around Gora).

The Tokyo EDITION, Ginza · 5 nights

2-8-13 Ginza, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 104-0061 · Chuo-dori / Higashi-Ginza corridor

  • Culture Kabuki-za — about 5–8 min walk; book one act while based here
  • Shop Itoya Ginza — stationery flagship (two buildings), ~5–10 min
  • Food Ginza sushi counters + Tsukiji outer market (~15–20 min) for lunch bowls
  • Food Depachika pastry: Mitsukoshi / Matsuya / Ginza Six food halls — short walks
  • Park Hibiya Park or Imperial Palace outer gardens — ~15–20 min west
  • Walk Back streets between Ginza and Higashi-Ginza (off Chuo-dori) for quieter loops
  • Sport Sports bars in Ginza / Yurakucho for Dodgers mornings; stadiums need a train
  • Train Ginza / Higashi-Ginza subway → Shinjuku for Odakyu Romancecar on Oct 14

Hakone Gora KARAKU · 2 nights

1300-8 Gora, Hakone-machi, Ashigarashimo-gun, Kanagawa 250-0408 · free shuttle ~3–5 min from Gora Station (hilly)

  • Onsen Private open-air bath in-room + rooftop / reservable baths on property — the stay’s centerpiece
  • Park Hakone Gora Park — roughly 10 min walk / short hop from Gora Station
  • Culture Hakone Open-Air Museum — one stop toward Chokoku-no-Mori / short walk
  • Train Gora Station: Hakone Tozan Railway + cable car toward Sounzan → ropeway / Lake Ashi loop
  • Food Lean on ryokan dinner/breakfast; one cafe lunch near Gora if you leave the property
  • Scenery Not dense city walking — plan ropeway / lake / pirate ship as the “day out,” onsen as the evening

Kyoto Machiya Fukune · 4 nights

105-3 Nishirokkakucho, Nakagyo-ku, Kyoto 604-8217 · central Nakagyo near Horikawa / Rokkaku

  • Food Nishiki Market — ~10 min; snacks, knives at Aritsugu
  • Walk Pontocho / Kiyamachi alleys — ~10–15 min for evening grazing
  • Culture Kyoto International Manga Museum / Karasuma corridor — short walk or one subway stop
  • Shop Stationery and kitchen tools around Nishiki / Teramachi covered streets
  • Park Kyoto Imperial Palace gardens — ~15–20 min north on foot or quick bus/subway
  • Food Neighborhood cafes for reading; bakery breakfasts before Arashiyama / Fushimi days
  • Train Easy subway / bus to Kyoto Station (Sagano, Shinkansen corridor) and Keihan for Fushimi

InterContinental Hotel Osaka · 3 nights

3-60 Ofuka-cho, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0011 · Grand Front Osaka North Building Tower C · Umeda (not Temmabashi / castle)

  • Train ~5 min walk to JR Osaka / Umeda / Hankyu — perfect for Kyoto day trips and Haruka via Shin-Osaka
  • Food Grand Front + Umeda depachika / izakaya density — okonomiyaki, kushikatsu, ramen without a long hop
  • Food Light Dotonbori is a short subway ride; prefer neighborhood Umeda food most nights
  • Walk Umeda Sky Building / floating garden (~10–15 min) for a view; Kita back streets off the malls
  • Shop Hankyu / Hanshin / Grand Front for pastry, kitchen gadgets, stationery browsing
  • Sport Umeda sports bars for MLB mornings; live NPB is elsewhere if schedules align
  • KIX Limousine bus stops near Umeda / Hankyu, or JR Haruka from Shin-Osaka (~10 min local from Osaka Sta.)

Money & trains

  • Cash: Pull yen at a 7-Eleven ATM with a US debit card (most reliable for American cards).
  • Translate: Google Translate camera mode before you land.
  • Walk more than the app suggests. Neighborhood streets between stations are often the point.
  • Ratings: Tabelog over Google; a 3.5 there can still be excellent.
  • Konbini: Onigiri, ATM, tickets. Carry trash — public bins are scarce.
  • Tickets: Suica/Pasmo for cities. Odakyu Romancecar Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto Oct 14. Shinkansen Odawara → Kyoto Oct 16 via SmartEX. Kyoto ↔ Osaka ordinary JR/Hankyu/Keihan. KIX Oct 23: Haruka from Shin-Osaka or limousine bus from Umeda.
  • Luggage: Forward Tokyo → Kyoto (or Osaka) with Yamato while overnighting Hakone with a small bag — or take bags on the Romancecar. Forward to KIX before departure if helpful.

Hakone notes

  • Luxury ryokan-style stay in Gora — midweek Oct 14–16 is a good window.
  • Romancecar Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto, then Tozan Railway to Gora + hotel shuttle.
  • Hakone Free Pass (Odakyu) or Romancecar reserved seat + local pay-as-you-go.
  • Onsen etiquette: Wash at the stools before entering; no swimsuits in traditional baths; hair up / towel off the water; quiet voices. Tattoos: confirm with KARAKU — in-room open-air baths solve most issues.

How to eat

Cap ramen at about four bowls. One proper sushi sit-down in Ginza, then sushi as lunch. Kyoto: kaiseki, tofu, obanzai, pastry. Osaka: savory — okonomiyaki, kushikatsu, izakaya. Use Tabelog as a tie-breaker.

Dodgers US night games = Japan morning (~8–11am). LCS ~Oct 12–20 Japan time if they advance. World Series Game 1 is Oct 23 US night — after you fly.
