# The Rabbit Hole

**An animated dark comedy short film, 3 minutes**

## ACT I: The Grind

**0:00 – 0:45 — The Magician's Partner**

A faded carnival tent at dusk. Peeling paint, broken lights, a handful of bored audience members scattered across rickety seats.

THE MAGICIAN (60s, threadbare tuxedo, tired eyes, still clutching his craft with worn hands) performs at his usual venue: a third-rate carnival that hasn't seen real crowds in decades. His stage presence is mechanical—the motions of a man performing muscle memory.

THE RABBIT (a white rabbit, intelligent and weary, wearing a tiny magician's coat) sits in a velvet cage, awaiting his cue.

**The Classic Routine:** The Magician pulls the Rabbit from an empty top hat to sparse applause. He places the Rabbit back in the hat and covers it with a silk cloth. The few audience members barely glance. The Magician taps the hat three times. Nothing changes. The Rabbit is still visibly inside—because he never left.

Backstage in a grimy dressing room, the Magician counts coins on a table. Barely enough for rent. The Rabbit smokes a cigarette in the corner (anthropomorphic, world-weary), staring at the mirror. Two survivors in a dying art form, going through the motions.

## ACT II: The Descent

**0:45 – 1:30 — Finding the Item / The Real Disappearance**

Late night. An alley behind the theater. Trash bins, graffiti, a single flickering street light.

The Rabbit sits alone, smoking, contemplating existence. Between two dumpsters, he spots something glowing faintly beneath the trash: a SMALL CRYSTALLINE OBJECT (undefined, magical, humming with energy). He picks it up. It's warm. It feels alive. He pockets it.

**The Next Show (Next Evening):**

Same terrible carnival. Slightly different audience. The Magician performs his routine again. This time, as he places the Rabbit in the hat and covers it with the silk cloth, something is different.

The Rabbit touches the magic item in his pocket. The hat glows.

The Magician taps it once. Twice. Third tap—

**The hat's bottom gives way.** The Rabbit plunges downward in a spiral of light, falling through layers of reality, into a vortex that is beautiful and disorienting.

The Magician stares into the hat. The Rabbit is gone. Truly gone. For the first time in his career, the trick works—and he doesn't know how or why.

## ACT III: The Rabbit Hole

**1:30 – 2:45 — Descent into Internet Chaos**

The Rabbit lands in the **Magical Realm**—a vast, Fantasia-scale world of living paintings and impossible physics. At first, it's wondrous.

**Stage 1 — The Wonderland (1:35 – 1:50):**
- Floating platforms of crystalline color
- Spiraling towers made of light and sound
- Creatures of pure imagination dancing in geometric patterns
- Everything is inviting, beautiful, surreal
- The Rabbit explores with childlike wonder

**Stage 2 — The Descent (1:50 – 2:30):**
The world begins to shift. The beauty corrupts into absurdity and madness.

*The Flat Earth Zone:* Mountains bend impossibly, horizons tilt. Figures argue about gravity while standing upside-down. The Rabbit watches in confusion.

*The TikTok Spiral:* Humanoid shapes repeat the same dance move endlessly, faster and faster, multiplying like a glitch. Their smiles are too wide. The Rabbit tries to escape but the dancers block his path, all doing the same gesture over and over.

*Area 51 District:* A cavernous space filled with conspiracy boards, blinking lights, shadowy figures searching for "the truth" in darkness. Voices whisper overlapping theories. The Rabbit is lost in the noise.

*The Instagram Kitchen:* A grotesque kitchen where influencers prepare bizarre, inedible recipes—dishes that defy logic and taste. Melting food, impossible combinations, all presented with forced enthusiasm. The Rabbit recoils.

*The Vortex of Endless Scrolling:* The world becomes a blur of images, each pulling the Rabbit deeper. He's drowning in content. The scale expands—Fantasia-level, but now twisted and oppressive.

**Stage 3 — The Exit Dance (2:30 – 2:45):**
The entire realm convulses. A pair of **enormous magical fingers** descend from above—like the hand of some digital god—and begins swiping across the world. With each swipe, things disappear, are reorganized, are swiped away in that characteristic gesture of endless scrolling.

The Rabbit, traumatized, realizes: *This entire world is the internet.*

The fingers swipe faster. The Rabbit, desperate and panicked, spots the glowing top hat in the distance—his only way home.

## ACT IV: Escape & Epilogue

**2:45 – 3:00 — The Return & The Reckoning**

The Rabbit runs toward the hat and dives back through it, gasping, collapsing onto the stage.

The Magician stands frozen, shocked. The Rabbit emerges, terrified, shaking, desperate. The audience applauds—they think it's the greatest trick they've ever seen.

But the Magician sees the truth in the Rabbit's eyes: *this was real.*

**Epilogue (final 15 seconds, voiceover or wordless):**
- The Magician examines the magic item, now inert
- He looks at the Rabbit, who shakes his head violently: *never again*
- The Rabbit refuses to get back in the hat
- Final shot: The Magician and Rabbit, older, years later, running a small bookshop or quiet coffee shop—away from the stage, away from the illusions
- But on a high shelf, the magic item sits, still glowing faintly
- The implication: *It's still out there. Always waiting for someone to find it again.*
